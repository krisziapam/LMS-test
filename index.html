FAQ

Login help made easier to scan

Important access instructions are grouped into accordions instead of one long block of text.

Go to the eMabini LMS portal, select Log in, then enter your assigned student or faculty credentials.

For students, use your student number. For faculty, use your assigned faculty number.

Use the temporary password provided by the University, then update it immediately after logging in.

Use Forgot Password on the login page. If no email arrives, contact eMabini support through official channels.
